Home Monitor on your home screen

Install the app for its own icon and window. Use an internet connection to load cameras and activity. Offline, the app opens its interface without saving camera or face images on this device.

Notifications on this device

Receive person alerts while alerts are on, and camera offline alerts at any time, even with the app closed. Tapping an alert opens that event. An alert not delivered within 15 minutes is dropped, and the event stays in Activity. On iPhone or iPad, enable notifications from the installed Home Screen app.

Alerts contain no images or people’s names. Internet access is needed for delivery; open the app to enable notifications and review activity.

Your data stays under your control

The collector sends selected snapshots to your recognition server over an encrypted Tailscale connection. Recognition runs on your server. Optional USB recording keeps video near the cameras and uploads alarm clips for review. Camera SD recording remains independent.

No face-recognition API, third-party analytics, or public camera links are used. This dashboard requires an activated device, or your private owner connection.
